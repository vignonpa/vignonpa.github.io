I am a PhD student working at the Laboratoire de Planétologie et Géosciences (LPG, UMR 6112) in Nantes, France.

My research focuses on Martian surface processes, particularly the seasonal dynamics linked to the sublimation of CO2 ice. I combine high-resolution satellite imagery analysis with modern computer vision techniques (Deep Learning, instance segmentation, YOLO) to process large-scale planetary datasets (CTX, HiRISE, CaSSIS, MOC, HRSC).

## Research Themes

- **Martian Surface Cryospheric Processes:** Study of the sublimation of the seasonal CO2 polar cap and its morphological signatures (dark spots, dark fans, araneiforms).
- **Computer Vision Applied to Planetary Science:** Development of Deep Learning pipelines (YOLO) for the automated detection and segmentation of geological features from orbital imagery (multi-instrumental data).
- **Atmosphere-Surface Interactions:** Analysis of the feedbacks between sublimation processes and the global Martian dust cycle.
- **Space Remote Sensing:** Exploitation of multi-scalar and multi-spectral data on martian polar regions (CTX, HiRISE, CaSSIS, MOC, HRSC, CRISM, MOLA...) and icy moons of the outer solar system (Cassini radar, VIMS, ISS).
